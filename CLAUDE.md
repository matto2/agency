# Project Guidelines

## Copy & terminology

- Say "parent pages" (or "parent-page"), never "master pages", when referring to InDesign page templates. Adobe renamed Master Pages to Parent Pages in InDesign 2022. Unrelated industry terms that use "master" (e.g. "master carton" in packaging) stay as they are.
- Italicize *California* when it refers to *California* magazine (`<em>California</em> magazine`). Keep the `<em>` and the neighboring words on the same source line, or the build drops the space next to it.
